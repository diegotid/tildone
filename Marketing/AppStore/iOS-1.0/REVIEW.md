# Tildone iPhone 1.0 — App Store copy

Prepared 25 October 2026. English (US), Spanish (Spain), French and Simplified Chinese. Local drafts for review; nothing has been uploaded or published. Previews and screenshots are outside this document’s scope.

## How to use this document

Copy only the text inside each field’s code block. Character counts include spaces, punctuation and internal line breaks, excluding a final file newline. Promotional text fits 170 characters; descriptions fit 4,000 characters; names and subtitles fit 30 characters each. Keywords conservatively fit both 100 characters and 100 UTF-8 bytes because Apple’s two guidance pages use different units.

**Name and subtitle are shared app information across platforms in the same App Store Connect record.** The iPhone and Mac targets share the bundle ID `studio.cuatro.tildone`; this document therefore preserves the four approved name/subtitle pairs from the macOS v3 package. These are not independent iOS overrides. The shared Chinese subtitle’s desktop reference describes the Mac side of the product; the phone description distinguishes the iPhone experience from desktop notes on Mac.

Promotional text, description and keywords are platform-specific. The iPhone copy puts capturing tasks, finding the next thing and continuing on Mac ahead of desktop benefits. It does not promise floating notes, gathering, dimming or background-transparency controls on iPhone.

**What’s New is not applicable to the first iPhone 1.0 release.** Apple does not provide that field for the first version of a platform. Each language section marks it as not applicable rather than inventing update notes. The Mac 2.0 release notes should not be reused here.

## Editorial choices and product checks

- The first sentence sells the outcome of getting a task out of your head; the opening also establishes free Mac–iPhone sync.
- iPhone can be used on its own. Mac continuity is a benefit, not a prerequisite. Sync requires the same Apple Account for iCloud on both devices; offline editing syncs after reconnection, without a promise of instantaneous delivery.
- List, grid and card-deck views, six note colors, title/task search, bracket tags, recent-action undo and progress are supported by the iOS implementation. Completed checklists leave the active overview. This copy does not describe an archive or promise the Mac’s timed deletion animation on iPhone.
- Ordinary unlimited checklists, the three overview layouts, note colors, search and iCloud sync are free. The iPhone Pro capabilities described here are subtasks, single memos and text styling. Pro is an optional one-time purchase shared with Mac, without a subscription.
- Keywords assume the exact shared names/subtitles shown. In English, `sticky` and `notes` are already in the subtitle; `memo` is in the keyword field. The iOS keyword field covers checklist, subtask, note-taking, capture, tags and offline intent instead of Mac-specific floating-window behavior. No shopping-related terms or Post-it brand variants have been added.
- Keyword demand and conversion gains have not been measured. These are relevant starting hypotheses, not guaranteed ranking improvements. The colon is an editorial choice, not a claimed ranking advantage. No unverified notification, due-date, widget, encryption or data-collection claims appear in the copy.
